About TempMal.com

TempMal.com is a free temporary email service built on top of the official Mail.tm API. Our goal is to give users a fast, simple way to create disposable email addresses for receiving messages without exposing their real inbox.

We prioritize privacy, simplicity, and reliability. Every mailbox is created in real time through Mail.tm, and we never store or expose your credentials in the browser.

Whether you need a temporary email for verification, sign-ups, or one-time downloads, TempMal.com offers an instant, no-registration experience.
