Privacy Policy

TempMal.com respects your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our temporary email service.

TempMal.com does not require registration. The only information created is a temporary mailbox address through the Mail.tm service. We do not collect personal information, names, or real email addresses.

Use of Temporary Mailboxes

Mailboxes are created via Mail.tm. We recommend not using temporary email for sensitive communications. Mailboxes may be removed after periods of inactivity according to Mail.tm policies.

We use browser local storage only to save your temporary session and theme preference. No tracking cookies are used.

Third-Party Service

TempMal.com uses Mail.tm for temporary email hosting. Please review Mail.tm's privacy policy for details about how they handle data.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Continued use of TempMal.com after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
